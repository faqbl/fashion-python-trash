8 puzzle problem requires to move number 1 to 8 in a 3×3 matrix.All the number blocks can only move towards the 4 directions without out of boundary,overlapping with or going across other blocks,which means we can only move the blocks near the blank.
In the repository,there are two program using different algorithm.One uses a star while another uses bfs.Both of them perform well,at least they can find the best solution.A star is supposed to be the better one.
These two program support custom-designed target,instead of only from small to big order.
